# Marianeczek

Marianeczek is a family app for people with type 1 diabetes who use AAPS and Nightscout. Each family installs it on **its own server** (for example a Mikrus VPS) – there is no shared central server and the author does not receive any family data.

It shows glucose from the family's Nightscout, keeps meal photos and notes, sends reminders (e.g. an old infusion site) and can store a daily backup of the family's own data on the family's own Google Drive.

**Marianeczek is not a medical device.** Treatment decisions are made by the person with diabetes and their doctor.
